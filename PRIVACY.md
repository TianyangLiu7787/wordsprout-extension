# WordSprout Privacy Policy

WordSprout is a browser extension for English learning. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how it is used.

## Information stored on your device

WordSprout stores your saved words and phrases, translations, notes, lookup cache, and display and site preferences in the browser's extension storage. When you save vocabulary from a webpage, WordSprout may also save that page's title and URL with the item so you can return to the source. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to the WordSprout developer. You can delete saved entries in WordSprout or clear the extension's data through your browser.

## Information sent to online services

When you look up text, WordSprout sends only the text needed for that lookup to the relevant service:

- MyMemory Translation API receives text that needs online translation.
- Free Dictionary API may receive an English word to retrieve its definition, pronunciation, or examples.
- Tatoeba may receive an English word to find bilingual example sentences.

These requests are sent directly from your browser to the named providers over HTTPS. Those providers may process request data, including network information, under their own policies. WordSprout does not operate a server that collects these queries. If the browser's on-device translation is available, it may be used as a local fallback.

PDF files opened in WordSprout's PDF reader are processed in the browser and are not uploaded. If you look up text selected from a PDF, that selected text is handled by the relevant lookup service as described above.

## Information WordSprout does not collect

WordSprout does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, health information, location, or full browsing history. It does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to English learning. It does not run analytics or tracking.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if WordSprout's data practices change. Questions can be submitted through the [WordSprout GitHub repository](https://github.com/TianyangLiu7787/wordsprout-extension/issues).
